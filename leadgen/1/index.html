What founders ask before they start

How much of my time does this take?

About an hour to write the qualification bar with me. After that, the only time you spend is in the meetings themselves. A 20-minute meeting with a $20,000 buyer is the best hour you'll have that week.

What if the meetings are bad?

You only pay for meetings that match the bar we wrote together. One that doesn't is credited. One that no-shows is rescheduled and not billed. You can't be billed for a bad meeting.

Don't I need volume?

If your deals are $20,000 and up, 3 right meetings a month beat 30 wrong ones. The agencies that promise 10 to 60 a month staff them with reps who don't know your product. That's the thing their own customers complain about most.

Why not an agency, or a hire?

An agency charges a retainer before a meeting exists and puts a rep on your account who has never used your product. A hire costs a salary and 3 months of ramp. The pilot costs $99 to find out, and you pay the rest only when a meeting happens.

Why does it matter that an engineer does the outreach?

Because the people who evaluate a dev tool are usually technical. When one asks what your tool does to their deploy pipeline, a rep says "great question, let me loop in the team." An engineer answers it. That answer is the difference between a reply and silence.
