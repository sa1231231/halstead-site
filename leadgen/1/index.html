How it works

  1. Who counts

    We write down who counts. Title, team size, what they run, and a sign they're in the market now. A job post. A migration.

  2. Find them

    I find the engineers who fit and read what they ship. Each one gets a note: who they are, what they run, why now.

  3. Write to them

    One engineer to another. No pitch. I've run their stacks, so I can answer the real questions. A few replies tell us if they meet the bar.

  4. Your calendar

    When they meet the bar, I put 20 minutes on your calendar and send you a short brief. You take the meeting. You close it.
